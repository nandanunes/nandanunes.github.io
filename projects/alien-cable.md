*Alien Cable is an interactive game for two players. Each player has a responsibility and can’t see the other player’s screen, like in Keep Talking Nobody Explodes. They need to work together to build a better alien cable TV. The game was developed during the Global Game Jam 2018, in São Paulo, Brazil.*

![](alien-cable-hero|16:9)

## The game

The theme of GGJ ’18 was Transmission. Among lots of other cool ideas – from virus killing people to satellites talking to each other while in orbit – our team decided to use the following concepts of transmission:

- Transmitting information
- Broadcasting

We combined both concepts and created Alien Cable: a game about TV Shows that need to use the players’ communication skills to be played.

Inspired by Keep Talking Nobody Explodes, Alien Cable needs two players to be played. The player one controls the Censor Console: an interface containing the TV Show Script, real time social media comments box, buttons to perform the censoring task and a meter that measures the quality of the show (according to the aliens’ expectations).

The player two receives a manual full of instructions and details on how the TV show (the one controlled by player one) needs to be censored. Both players need to communicate during the game to achieve their goal: build a better Cable TV for the Aliens, censoring human shows.
