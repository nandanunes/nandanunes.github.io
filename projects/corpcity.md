*Corpcity is a free-to-play 3X RTS about building a corporation empire from scratch – you start with a lemonade stand! All that while competing real time against other players.*

![](corpcity-hero|16:9)

## Overview

I joined the **Corpcity** team as a **Senior UX Designer**, responsible for improving the gameplay experience and leading the redesign of all social and metagame features. The game was canceled before soft-launch.

### What I did

- Benchmarked the main references for the upcoming features
- Planned and conducted workshops within the team to plan new features and remove design or product barriers
- Prototyped screens and interactions using Figma and Principle
- Redesigned the Metagame experience related to purchasing, selling and tuning of the products and prices of a business
- Redesigned the “Managers” feature, an extra layer of complexity in the game design that gives boosts to a specific business and debuffs to nearby businesses
- Redesigned the “Open Market” feature, where Players could sell their surpluses and also purchase from others
