*Coup: Dinosaurs and the 2nd World War is a re-skin of the famous card game Coup. The game was made in 2019 to give as a present in a DIY Secret Santa event.*

![](coup-01|16:9)

## The idea

In December 2019 I participated in a Secret Santa event at work where you needed to build your gift. In other words, it was forbidden to buy a ready-made gift from a store. Since there were almost 50 people participating, someone had the idea to set up a Trello kanban board so we could all write our likes and dislikes, giving some clues for the gift making process. We had only 3 weeks to create something.

My Secret Santa was someone into Dinosaurs, World History (especially the 2nd World War) and games. I tried to mix his interests and use my Graphic Design skills to build something useful and fun. Then I had the idea: “why not re-skin a game themed with the subjects he likes?”

And here is the result: **Coup: Dinosaurs and 2nd World War Edition.**
