*Nasty Island is a card game made during the 48 hour event Global Game Jam 2017 at University of Southern California (Los Angeles).*

![](nasty-island-hero|16:9)

The Global Game Jam theme in 2017 was Waves. I teamed up with my wife to try to make a game together. Since we didn’t have good coding skills, we decided to design a board game.

## Design process

Our brainstorming on the word “Waves” brought us interesting insights such as:

- Hair waves
- Movement
- Circle
- Sound waves as navigation
- Sea waves
- Waves bring stuff
- Washed ashore ← the concept behind the game

After picking the main element to inspire the game, the hard task was to bring the concept into something real and playable. **How were we going to show the players the wave movement of “washed ashore”?**

We found the answer in the game mechanic. After shuffling our deck made of things that are “washed ashore”, we divide the deck into three piles. Imitating the wave movement, the player needs to reveal one card from each pile. The back of the cards is the sea, and when the card is revealed the player finds out what is left at the beach.

![The game setup: three decks and three cards revealed.](nasty-island-01|16:9)

After defining the game flow we started to add more game elements:

- **Washed ashore items.** They have a point value ranging from 2 to 5
- **Cards that can mess your turn.** Oil Spill prevents the player from drawing a card
- **Cards worth 0 points.** Sometimes the sea only brings... sand
- **Characters with distinct characteristics.** The player has a specific bonus during the game

## Characters

Each of the four playable characters has its own special power.

![](nasty-island-02|16:9)

- **Ambergris Master.** You score 1+ points for every Ambergris card you collect
- **Artifact/Organic Dealer.** You score 1+ points for every Artifact or Organic card in greater number that you collect
- **Sand Crafter.** You can only pick Sand cards, and you score 1x the number of Sand cards
- **Oil Mutant.** You are not affected by the Oil Spill card

### Washed ashore stuff

Starfish, Shell, Seaweed, Beach Ball, Bottle, Rubber Duck, Ambergris, Giant Ambergris, King Rubber Duck, Weird Creature, Oil Spill and Sand.

## Playtest

Due to the limited time, the game was not playtested enough. However, we are very happy with the final result.

![A playtesting session with one of the game jam participants.](nasty-island-03|16:9)

## The story

“*In this tiny island where no dreams come true, your only chance of hope is..... scavenging through the waves! Every set of wave brings three amusing stuff that gives you the opportunity to collect and show off to your friends how cool you are on this island that you probably will soon ebb away (of dehydration). There’s some of that fancy role playing stuff in it too because why keep sanity on an island for two? Now the question is which one are you going to be? The Artifact/Organic Dealer, Sand Crafter, Ambergris Master or simply the Oil Mutant?”*
