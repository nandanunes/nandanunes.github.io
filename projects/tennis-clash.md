## Overview

I worked in the Tennis Clash team as the **UX Designer**, responsible for two new important social features:

- **Clubs**, a feature analogous to Clans. I worked mostly on **solving corner cases** and **playtesting**.
- **Club Slam**, the tennis version of Clan Wars. I was part of the **conception of the visual design handoff**.

### What I did

- Benchmarking the main references for the upcoming feature
- Planning and conducting workshops within the team to plan the new feature and remove design or product barriers
- Prototyping the flow and screens using Figma
- Designing the whole feature and how it would interact with already established features such as Clubs and Friends
- Playtest with new and seasoned players to gather their feedback, especially on how we inform them about their performance during the competition
- Documenting the whole UX process along with the PM.

:::track Club Slam
The **Club Slam** was created to improve the social experiences in Tennis Clash, connecting the features Clubs and Friends. The mechanics are very **similar to Clan Wars from games like Clash Royale**, with an extra **twist**: players have to consider the **different tennis courts** to plan their strategy.

The **biggest UX challenge** was to find a clear way to show the players how their team was doing (the Score Display), while keeping them engaged with the competition and informed about points and prizes.

### UX process

1. Align the UX vision of the feature along with the Game Design and Product specs
2. Workshop with the main stakeholders to get feedback and insights from different perspectives regarding the feature’s design (product, engineering, design, art)
3. Wireframe the flow and main screens
4. Playtest with different players the core loop of the feature, collecting feedback on creating decks, finishing the matches, cooldown of the characters and performance of the Club
5. Follow the visual design and art concept process so it’s aligned with the product vision

:::track Clubs: Cheer Up
**Clubs** is a social feature that allows players to create groups with friends and other players to chat, exchange strategies, play friendly matches so they complete their daily challenges and help each other open their loot bags.

I worked mostly on **solving corner cases** and **play testing** the feature for further improvements. My main contribution was **improving the Cheer Up experience**, a feature inside Clubs that allowed players to interact with each other in order to decrease their loot bags opening time.

### UX process

1. Study the feature, plotting the UX flow and stressing corner cases
2. Alignment with product to ensure the vision of the feature
3. Alignment with engineering to guarantee all scenarios were going to be considered
4. Play test the feature before its release
