COUP: Dinosaurs and 2nd WW Ed

Coup: Dinosaurs and the 2nd World War is a re-skin of the famous card game Coup. The game was made in 2019 to give as present in a DIY Secret Santa event.

Role
Visual Designer
Occasion
Personal Project
Year
2019

The idea

In December 2019 I participated in a Secret Santa event at work where you needed to build a gift. In other words, it was forbidden to buy a ready-made gift from a store. Since there were almost 50 people participating in the game, someone had the idea to set up a kanban board (Trello) so we could all write our likes and dislikes, giving some clues for the gift making process. We had only 3 weeks to create something.

My Secret Santa was someone into Dinosaurs, World History (especially 2nd World War) and games. I tried to mix his interest and use my Graphic Design skills to build something useful and fun. Then I had the idea "why not re-skin a game themed with the subjects he likes?".

And here is the result: Coup: Dinosaurs and 2nd World War Edition.
