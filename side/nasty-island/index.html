Nasty Island

Role
Game Design, Product Management, Visual Designer
Occasion
Global Game Jam
Year
2017

Nasty Island is a card game made during the 48 hour event Global Game Jam 2017 at University of Southern California (Los Angeles).

The Global Game Jam theme in 2017 was Waves. I teamed up with my wife to try to make a game together. Since we didn't have good coding skills, we decided to design a board game.

Design Process

Our brainstorming on the word “Waves” brought us interesting insights such as:

  • Hair waves
  • Movement
  • Circle
  • Sound waves as navigation
  • Sea waves
  • Waves bring stuff
  • Washed ashore < the concept behind the game

After picking the main element to inspire the game the hard task was to bring the concept into something real and playable.

How are we going to show the players the wave movement of “washed ashore”?

We found the answer in the game mechanic. After shuffling our deck made of things that are “washed ashore”, we divide the deck into three piles. Imitating the wave movement, the player needs to reveal one card from each deck. The back of the cards is the sea and when the card is revealed the player finds out what is left at the beach.

Game mechanic: three decks and three cards revealed.

After defining the game flow we started to add more game elements as:

  • Washed Ashore items: they have point value ranging from 2 to 5.
  • Cards that can mess your turn: Oil Spill prevents the player from drawing a card.
  • Cards value 0 points: sometimes the sea only brings... sand.
  • Characters with distinct characteristics: the player has a specific bonus during the game.

Characters

Ambergris Master: you score 1+ points for every Ambergris card you collect.

Artifact/Organic Dealer: you score 1+ points for every Artifact or Organic cards in greater number that you collect.

Sand Crafter: you can only pick Sand cards. You score 1x number of the Sand cards.

Oil Mutant: you are not affected by Oil Spill card.

Due to the limited time, the game was not play tested enough. However, we are very happy with the final result.

Nasty Island Story

by Nil Şimşek

"In this tiny island where no dreams come true, your only chance of hope is..... scavenging through the waves! Every set of wave brings three amusing stuff that gives you the opportunity to collect and show off to your friends how cool you are on this island that you probably will soon ebb away (of dehydration). There’s some of that fancy role playing stuff in it too because why keep sanity on an island for two? Now the question is which one are you going to be? The Artifact/Organic Dealer, Sand Crafter, Ambergris Master or simply the Oil Mutant?"
