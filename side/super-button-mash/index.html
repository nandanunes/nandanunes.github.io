Super Button Mash

Role
Game Design, Visual Designer
Occasion
SP Jam
Year
2011

Super Button Mash is a card game that simulates arcade fighting games.

The game was made in 2012 at SPJam: 48 hour Game Jam in São Paulo - Brazil.

The game

The players start with 20 health points and can choose one character to be the fighter. To attack, the player needs to discard the cards that match with some sequence (move) of the character's card. The card "Button Mash" allows the player to pick one of the moves to use without discarding cards from her hand. The first player to win two rounds is the winner.

  • 4 character cards
  • 35 move cards (arrows and letters)
  • 3 Button Mash cards

My role was designing some of the cards, the box and helping with playtesting.
