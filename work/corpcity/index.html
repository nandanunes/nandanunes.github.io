Corpcity

Corpcity is a free-to-play 3X RTS about building a corporation empire from scratch – you start with a lemonade stand! All that while competing real time against other players.

Role
UX

I joined the Corpcity team as a Senior UX Designer, responsible for improving the gameplay experience and leading the re-designing all social and metagame features.

My responsibilities

  • Benchmarking of main references for the upcoming features;
  • Planned and conducted workshops within the team to plan new features and remove design or product barriers;
  • Prototyped screens and interactions using Figma and Principle;
  • Re-designed the Metagame experience related to purchasing, selling and tuning of the products and prices of a business;
  • Re-designed the “Managers” feature, an extra layer of complexity in the game design that gives boosts to a specific business and debuffs to nearby business;
  • Re-design the “Open Market” feature, where Players could sell their surpluses and also purchase from others;

The game was canceled before soft-launch.
